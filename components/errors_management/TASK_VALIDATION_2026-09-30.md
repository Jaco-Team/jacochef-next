## Source semantics decision

`source` is the **complaint registration channel**, not the origin of the order. It intentionally contains only `contact_center` and `cafe`, which satisfies the task requirement to keep Contact Center and Café statistics separate.

The task separately requires the order origin—client, Contact Center, cashier, or another source—and employee identity where applicable, inside order details. That projection is missing and must be added without turning website orders into a third complaint-statistics source.
